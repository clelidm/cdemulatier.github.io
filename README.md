<p style="padding: 0px 22px 0px">I am an assistant professor at the University of Amsterdam (UvA), where I work at the <a href="https://iop.uva.nl/itfa/itfa.html" target="_blank" rel="no opener noopener">Institute for Theoretical Physics</a> and at the <a href="https://ivi.uva.nl" target="_blank" rel="no opener noopener">Informatics Institute</a> as a member of the <a href="https://iop.fnwi.uva.nl/computational_soft_matter/" target="_blank" rel="no opener noopener">Computational Soft Matter Lab</a> and of the <a href="https://uva.computationalscience.nl" target="_blank" rel="no opener noopener">Computational Science Lab</a>. I am affiliated with the <a href="https://www.d-iep.org/" target="_blank" rel="no opener noopener">Dutch Institute for Emergent Phenomena</a>.</p>

<p style="padding: 0px 22px 0px">My research lies at the intersection of statistical physics, information theory and computer science, and focuses on the development of new theoretical methods to study complex systems. My research is both theoretical and numerical. I also collaborate with experimentalists in neuroscience and in biology.</p>

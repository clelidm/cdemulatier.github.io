<span style="font-size: medium"><strong>Bachelor students looking for a Master program:</strong></span>
<li><strong>Physics Students interested in the Physics of Complex Systems:</strong>
<p style="padding: 0px 22px 0px">
You may be interested in the Master Physics and Astronomy at UvA/VU with a <strong>specialization in <a href="https://www.uva.nl/shared-content/programmas/en/masters/physics-and-astronomy-general-physics-and-astronomy/physics-and-astronomy-general-physics-and-astronomy.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">"Computational Physics of Complex Systems"</a> (CPCS)</strong>. The program is currently available under the <a href="https://studiegids.uva.nl/xmlpages/page/2022-2023-en/search-programme/programme/7160/247913" target="_blank" rel="noopener">track "General Physics and Astronomy"</a> (GPA).
You can find <a href="https://iop.fnwi.uva.nl/computational_soft_matter/msc_route_gpa_cpcs.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a> is more information on the program, and the list of the courses we recommend to students following the specialization.
</p>
<li><strong>Students with a Scientific background interested in Computational Science and in Complex Systems:</strong>
<p style="padding: 0px 22px 0px">

You can find more information about the program <a href="https://www.uva.nl/en/programmes/masters/computational-science/computational-science.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a> and the possible application domains <a href="https://www.uva.nl/en/programmes/masters/computational-science/study-programme/graduation-domains.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a> (at the bottom of the page).
